Analyze our Q3 sales and tell me what I should do to sell more and improve margins.

  • Pulled Q3 sales from the ERP
  • Cross-referenced customers and opportunities in the CRM
  • Checked inventory and the demand forecast

I reviewed July to September sales for all 40 distributors, together with the customer base and inventory. It was a good quarter, but there are four clear opportunities to close out the year.

Quarterly sales
$11.7M
▲ 8.4% year over year
Gross margin
31.2%
▼ 0.9 pp in wholesale
Active customers
2,318
▼ 38 stopped buying

What I found

  • The north accounts for almost half of the growth: three distributors grew more than 20% with the new snack line.
  • 38 customers who used to buy every month didn’t buy in September. They represent $420K in monthly sales.
  • The snack line reaches only 35% of points of sale, versus 61% for the potato chip line.
  • Wholesale discounts rose 2.3 points and margin fell 0.9 pp.

What I recommend

  1. 1

    Call the 38 at-risk customers this week This week

    I built the list by sales rep, sorted by revenue.

    Recover up to $420K a month

  2. 2

    Take the snack line to the west and south Next 30 days

    There are 650 points of sale that buy chips but not snacks yet.

    +$1.1M estimated per quarter

  3. 3

    Cap wholesale discounts This month

    Going back to June levels restores margin without hurting volume.

    +0.9 pp margin

  4. 4

    Restock 3 products before October 15 Urgent

    The forecast predicts stockouts of Classic Chips 400 g, Fries 1 kg and Mashed Potatoes 125 g.

    Avoid losing $180K in sales

Should I send each sales rep their customer list with the suggested product for their next visit?

  • Yes, build the lists
  • Project the October close
  • Show me the west in detail
