The problem

You’re not short on data. You’re short on data that’s ready for decisions.

Scattered information

Sales in the ERP, inventory in one spreadsheet, targets in another. Every department has its own version of the numbers.

A single data model that updates itself.

Reports that arrive too late

Every month-end close means days of copying, pasting and checking data by hand. By the time the report is ready, it’s too late to act.

Dashboards ready on the first day of the month, not the fifth.

Flying blind

Without clear KPIs, it’s hard to tell which customer, product or channel is underperforming, and why.

Alerts and recommendations when something goes off track.
