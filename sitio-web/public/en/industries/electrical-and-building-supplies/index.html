The challenges we see

What holds companies like yours back

01

Catalogs with thousands of items

Duplicate codes, different descriptions for the same product and cross-brand equivalents make inventory and purchasing harder.

02

Price lists that change all the time

Every supplier update means recalculating prices, and it’s easy to sell below replacement cost without noticing.

03

Orders by WhatsApp and email

Contractors and job sites order by message, with photos and informal descriptions, and someone has to interpret them and enter them.

04

Customers who buy only part of the range

An electrician or a construction company buys cable from you but not lighting or circuit protection, and nobody notices in time.

KPIs

What we measure

We define the key KPIs with your team and calculate them every day with the same rules.

  • Sales and margin by family, brand and customer
  • Selling price vs. replacement cost
  • Discounts by customer and sales rep
  • Inventory, turnover and slow-moving items
  • Stockouts
  • Cross-selling by product family
  • Active, new and inactive customers
  • Customer accounts and days to collect
  • Order picking and delivery times

FAQ

What we’re often asked

Have another question? Write to us and let’s talk it through.

Our catalog is a mess. Can we still start?

Yes. Cleaning up the item master is usually the first step, and we do it with rules that then apply automatically to new items.

Does the agent understand informally written orders?

That’s the idea: it interprets descriptions like the ones your customers use and matches them to your catalog. We always start with a pilot, and a person reviews each order before it’s confirmed.

Where do we start?

With a data assessment: in two weeks you’ll know which sources you have and which solution to build first.
