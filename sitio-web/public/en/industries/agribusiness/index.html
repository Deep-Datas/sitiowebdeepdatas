The challenges we see

What holds companies like yours back

01

Highly seasonal sales

Demand for inputs is concentrated in a few weeks each season, and a planning mistake costs dearly in inventory or lost sales.

02

Customer accounts that are hard to track

Sales paid at harvest, barter deals, different currencies and long terms make it time-consuming to know how much each grower owes you.

03

Information in spreadsheets and in each rep’s head

What each customer plants, how many hectares they farm and what they bought last season is often not in any system.

04

Inventory spread across plants and warehouses

Inputs and grain in several locations, with movements recorded late.

KPIs

What we measure

We define the key KPIs with your team and calculate them every day with the same rules.

  • Sales and margin by season, crop and region
  • Share of each grower’s purchases
  • New, repeat and lost customers
  • Balances and days to collect by grower
  • Overdue debt
  • Input and grain inventory by plant
  • Target attainment by sales rep
  • Harvest-payment sales and barter deals

FAQ

What we’re often asked

Have another question? Write to us and let’s talk it through.

Can you work with sales in pesos and in dollars?

Yes. We agree with your team on how to convert and compare amounts (for example, using each transaction’s exchange rate) and always apply the same rule.

A lot of information is in each sales rep’s spreadsheets. Can we use it?

Yes. We unify those spreadsheets with clear rules and, if it helps, propose a simple way to record each grower’s data so it doesn’t depend on one person.

Where do we start?

With a data assessment: in two weeks you’ll know which sources you have and which solution to build first.
