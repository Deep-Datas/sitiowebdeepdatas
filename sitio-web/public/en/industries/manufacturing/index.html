What holds companies like yours back

01

Costs nobody fully knows

With raw material prices changing often, it’s hard to know the real cost and margin of each product.

02

Planning production by gut feel

Without a sales forecast, you overproduce what doesn’t sell and run short of what does.

03

Raw materials that run out or pile up

Purchasing decisions are made at short notice, mixing rush orders, overstock and tied-up capital.

04

The plant and the office, disconnected

Output, waste and downtime are recorded on spreadsheets or paper and don’t reach decision-makers in time.

KPIs

What we measure

We define the key KPIs with your team and calculate them every day with the same rules.

  • Cost and margin by product and customer
  • Production plan attainment
  • Efficiency by line and shift
  • Scrap and waste
  • Downtime and its causes
  • Raw material and finished goods inventory
  • Forecast accuracy

  • On-time deliveries

FAQ

What we’re often asked

Have another question? Write to us and let’s talk it through.

Much of our plant records data on paper or in spreadsheets. Does that work?

Yes. We start with what you have and propose simple ways to capture the data (forms or spreadsheets with rules), without changing everything at once.

Do we need sensors or a plant system?

Not to get started. Many KPIs can be calculated from ERP data and production records. If adding automatic machine data makes sense later on, we’ll evaluate it.

Where do we start?

With a data assessment: in two weeks you’ll know which sources you have and which solution to build first.
