KPIs

What we measure

We define the key KPIs with your team and calculate them every day with the same rules.

  • Sales and margin by customer, product and sales rep
  • Profitability by business line
  • Inventory, turnover and slow-moving products
  • Accounts receivable and days to collect
  • New, active and inactive customers
  • Projected cash flow
  • Expenses by category vs. budget
  • Target attainment
