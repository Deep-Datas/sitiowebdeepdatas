Scattered data

Today your information is spread out and it’s hard to trust the numbers. Before thinking about artificial intelligence, it pays to build the foundation: integrate your main sources and define your key KPIs. It’s the most common starting point, and the one where an assessment pays off the most.

Your priorities

  1. Integration. Bring your main sources into a single model that updates itself. Without it, every analysis starts from scratch.
  2. Timeliness. Automate data loading and calculations so you have your KPIs on the first day of the month, not the fifth.
  3. Data governance. Agree on a single definition for each KPI (net sales, margin, active customer) and on who owns each one.
  4. Data quality. Clean up and unify your customer and product master data, with rules that catch errors before they reach your reports.
  5. Data-driven decisions. Put your key KPIs into dashboards that each person uses day to day, with alerts when something drifts off course.
  6. Artificial intelligence. Choose a focused, high-impact use case (sales questions, alerts or orders) and test it with curated data.

Your answers show a very solid foundation across every area. The next step is choosing the AI use case with the greatest impact on your business.
