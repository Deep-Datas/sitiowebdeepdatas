FAQ

Before you write to us

The questions we hear most. If yours isn’t here, send us a message.

Does my data need to be organized before we start?

No. Part of our work is integrating, cleaning and preparing the data: we start from the sources you already have (systems, spreadsheets, databases) and turn them into reliable information.

Which platforms do you work with?

Our solutions are platform-agnostic. We assess your infrastructure and needs to recommend the right tool; we usually work with Power BI, Azure, SQL and Python.

How big does my company need to be?

We work with organizations of different sizes and industries. What matters is not the volume of data, but having decisions that can be improved with information.

How do you keep information secure?

Security is one of our pillars: we use the best technologies and follow the protocols and regulations that apply to each industry and each client.

What is the Client portal?

It’s the private space where our clients and distributors view their dashboards and reports. You can sign in from the “Client portal” link in the footer, with the credentials we assign.

What is the first step?

Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll set up a first conversation to understand your challenge and then put together a tailored proposal.
