About us

Data consultants with a business mindset.

We are a data and artificial intelligence consultancy based in Buenos Aires. We integrate, organize and analyze data for companies in consumer goods, distribution and SMEs, and we build Vera: the assistant that turns all of it into answers.

Our mission

Making decisions with reliable data shouldn’t be a privilege reserved for large companies.

We deliver concrete strategies to solve each organization’s toughest challenges with data: not just for large corporations, but for any company that wants to make better decisions.

That’s why we combine technical expertise with a business perspective. Before we talk about tools, we make sure we understand the decisions you need to make.

Vera · AI assistant

We built Vera, and we stand behind every answer.

We are not reselling a generic chatbot. Vera is ours: we connect the assistant to your systems, teach it the rules of your business and answer for every number it gives. The mark is two sources converging on a single point, which is what all of this is about.

Ask the way you talk
Sales, stock, customers and targets, with no dashboards and no filters.
Answers with a source
Every number says where it comes from: the ERP, a spreadsheet or an email. Vera does not make things up.
Anticipates, and acts
Warns before a stockout, drafts the purchase order and loads the orders that arrive on WhatsApp.
