What you’ll get

A clear report to decide your next step

It’s not a generic slide deck: it’s an analysis of your real data, with conclusions you can use from day one.

Map of your data

Which sources you have (systems, spreadsheets, messages, files), what information each one holds and how it flows between departments.

Quality assessment

A score for each source: completeness, duplicates, consistency and freshness. You know exactly where the problems are.

AI readiness

Which artificial intelligence use cases are feasible today with your data, which ones aren’t yet and what it takes to get there.

Prioritized roadmap

The highest-impact opportunities, ranked by benefit and effort, with estimated timelines for each one.

How it works

Two weeks, four steps and little of your team’s time

  1. Week 1

    Kickoff meeting

    One hour to understand your business, your key decisions and your goals. We agree on which departments and sources to review.

  2. Week 1

    Discovery

    We review data samples or read-only access and hold short interviews with the people who use the information.

  3. Week 2

    Analysis

    We measure the quality of each source, map how information flows and assess the AI use cases.

  4. Week 2

    Results presentation

    We present the report and the roadmap, and answer all of your team’s questions.

It’s for you if…

  • Your information is spread across systems, spreadsheets and messages.
  • Reports take days of manual work and don’t always match.
  • You want to apply artificial intelligence but don’t know where to start.
  • You need to justify an investment in data with a concrete plan.

What we need from you

  • A point person to coordinate the discovery.
  • Data samples or read-only access to the main sources.
  • Between 4 and 6 hours of your team’s time, spread over the two weeks.

FAQ

What happens when the assessment is over?

You keep the report and the roadmap, and you decide how to move forward: implement it with your own team, with another provider or with us. There is no commitment to continue.

How much does it cost?

The scope and the fee are agreed in the first conversation, based on the number of sources and departments to review. You always know the price before we start.

How do you protect the confidentiality of my data?

We sign a confidentiality agreement before we start and work with read-only access or data samples.

My data lives in Excel and WhatsApp. Does that work?

Yes, that’s the most common case. The assessment identifies exactly which information is worth rescuing from those sources and how to integrate it.

Is it useful if we’re not thinking about AI yet?

Yes. The same curated data an AI agent needs is what makes your dashboards and reports reliable. The roadmap covers both.
