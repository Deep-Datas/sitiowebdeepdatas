Data engineering

What’s included

  • Automated, cross-platform ingestion pipelines
  • A centralized data model, ready for analysis
  • Load monitoring and error alerts

02

Data quality and preparation

Reliable data comes first: we detect errors, duplicates and gaps, and fix them with business rules and machine learning models.

What’s included

  • Quality assessment of every source
  • Cleaning, deduplication and standardization
  • Unified customer, product and distributor master data
  • Data governance: definitions, owners and shared criteria

OutcomeNumbers every department trusts, with documented criteria.

03

Analytics and predictive models

What’s included

  • Industry-standard KPIs
  • KPIs tailored to your business
  • Predictive models for demand, sales and customer behavior
  • Customer and point-of-sale segmentation

OutcomeKPIs that show where to act, and forecasts that let you decide sooner.
