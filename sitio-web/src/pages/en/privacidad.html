Privacy policy

What data we receive through this website, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Data controller

DeepDatas, located at Bernardo de Irigoyen 330, C1072 Autonomous City of Buenos Aires, Argentina. You can write to us at fbloise@deepdatas.com.

What data we receive

  • Contact, data assessment and AI readiness check forms: full name, email, company, phone number, the topic you are interested in and your message. The data is sent by email to our team; the website does not store it in a database.
  • AI readiness check: your answers are processed in your browser to show you the result and are not sent anywhere. We only receive them, together with the result, if you fill in your contact details at the end.
  • Usage statistics: we use Microsoft Clarity, configured without cookies, to understand in aggregate how the website is used (pages visited, clicks, scrolling, device type and approximate location).
  • Third-party services: the WhatsApp links and the Google map are governed by those companies’ privacy policies.

What we use it for

We use the form data only to answer your inquiry and arrange a conversation. We do not sell it or share it with third parties for commercial purposes. Usage statistics help us improve the website.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact details for as long as necessary to answer your inquiry and follow up on it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under Argentine Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326, you can access your data and ask us to correct, update or delete it by writing to fbloise@deepdatas.com. Access is free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless you can show a legitimate interest.

Argentina’s Agency for Access to Public Information (Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública), as the supervisory authority under Law No. 25,326, is empowered to handle complaints and claims filed by anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules in force.
