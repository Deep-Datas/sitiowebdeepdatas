Dashboard and question

A dashboard is for exploring. Vera is for asking.

They don’t compete: they do different jobs. When the question is specific — “which distributor is below target?”, “how much should I buy from my supplier?” — Vera answers it on WhatsApp, with the number and the source, and nobody opens anything. When you want to understand why that number is what it is, you open the dashboard and work through it. What no longer makes sense is opening it every morning to see whether you spot something.
